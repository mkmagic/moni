A Two insight styles

Toggle Briefing / Feed above. Briefing is one plain-language line with colored figures — reads like a morning brief, no button shapes. Feed is a quiet notification list: soft severity icon, text, and an inline "→ Budget" link on the right.

Both live inside a single panel, so it reads as content, not a toolbar. Tell me which you prefer (or a hybrid).

B Last sync, surfaced

Every panel carries a Last synced N days ago meta line with a "Sync now" link. When it goes stale (say > 7 days) it escalates from this quiet footer into a colored feed row — the same escalation the over-budget and categorize items use.

C Clear day: summary at the bottom

On a clear day the panel is gone from the top — net worth and the graphs lead, and the summary drops below as a calm "all caught up" footer. The Needs-categorizing card isn't rendered at all when the queue is empty.

D Which categories are over

The budget block links to /budget, and the over-budget categories are named inline with their overage — Groceries +₪120, Dining +₪80 — each a link to that category's transactions. No drilling to find out what's red.

Alternatives if you'd rather: (1) tiny per-category bars showing the overshoot, or (2) a dedicated "Over budget" mini-card. Say the word and I'll mock either.

E Still one net worth, grouped month

One net-worth hero (the duplicate grid card that plotted m.net is gone). Income, expenses and budget share one "This month" card; Income-vs-Expenses is a compact two-line sparkline, not a full area chart.

F Chip beside the name · mobile

The category suggestion sits right after the merchant. Switch to Mobile: the rail collapses, the health split stacks, and because the top is adaptive a busy phone opens onto the summary + queue, a clear one onto net worth.
